A great setup isn't about buying the most RGB. It's about a desk that keeps you comfortable, puts everything within reach and stays tidy enough that you actually enjoy sitting at it. Here's the order we'd build it in.

In this guide

1. Comfort: chair and posture

You'll spend more hours in your chair than with any other part of the setup. Look for adjustable lumbar support, seat height and armrests that line up with your desk so your elbows rest at about 90 degrees. Feet should sit flat on the floor; use a footrest if your desk is high.

3. Screen and lighting

Place the top of your monitor at or slightly below eye level, roughly an arm's length away. A high-refresh monitor makes fast games feel smoother, while a portable second screen is handy for chat, guides or streaming tools. A monitor light bar lights the keyboard area without reflecting on the screen, which is easier on your eyes during late sessions.

Buying advice

  1. Spend on what you touch and sit on. Chair, mouse, keyboard and headset first; decorative lighting last.
  2. Plan cable management early. A cable tray or a few hook-and-loop ties under the desk make a bigger visual difference than most accessories.
  3. Measure before you buy. Check your desk depth for monitor distance and mat size.
  4. Buy within your region. Local stores make warranty claims and returns much easier. Every APEXDRIP card shows the store's country and currency, and the regional pages collect offers for India, the USA, the UK, Chile and international stores.
