AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE.

Last updated 25 Nov 2026

APEXDRIP is an independent product discovery website. We are reader-supported: when you click some links on this site and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer or affiliate network. This never costs you anything extra — the price you pay is the same as going to the store directly.

How we label links

  • Affiliate link — the link contains a tracking code from an affiliate programme or network. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. These links are marked sponsored for search engines.
  • Retailer link — a direct link to the official store with no tracking code, used while affiliate approval for that store is pending. We don't earn from these links today, but they may be replaced with affiliate links in future.
  • Sponsored banners — the promotional banners on our department pages link to retailer offers through affiliate networks and are labelled as sponsored for assistive technology.

Which programmes we work with

We participate in, or have applied to, affiliate programmes run directly by brands and through affiliate networks. These include programmes for brands and stores featured on this site, such as Redragon, REDMAGIC, ASUS and Lenovo. Each programme has its own terms; we follow their rules on disclosure, coupon use and paid advertising.

How we choose products

Commission rates do not decide what we feature. We pick products from established brands, compare published specifications and features, and write our own descriptions and guides. We don't accept payment to place products in our buying guides. If a brand ever sponsors content, that content will be clearly labelled as sponsored.

Prices, availability and regions

We only show a price when we've checked it on the retailer's own site, and we display the date we checked it. Prices, stock, promotions and shipping can change at any time, and the retailer's checkout is always the final word. Every product shows the country and currency of the store that sells it; we never convert or mix currencies between regions.

Your purchase is with the retailer

APEXDRIP does not sell products, take payments or handle orders. When you buy, your contract is with the retailer, and their terms, privacy policy, shipping, returns and warranty apply.

Questions

If you have questions about this disclosure or a specific link, please contact us. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
